FAQ

How the data is put together and what it should be used for.

Where do the addresses come from?

City names, ZIP ranges and area codes are real. The house number and street name are combined at random from common US street names, so a generated street may or may not exist in that city.

Are the phone numbers real?

No. Every number uses the 555-0100 to 555-0199 block, which is reserved for fictional use, behind the city's real area code.

Are the email addresses real?

No. They all use example.com, a domain reserved for documentation, so test emails never reach a real inbox.

Why is there no SSN field?

Fake Social Security numbers are a common tool for identity fraud, and form testing works just as well with a clearly fake placeholder. Use 000-00-0000 if your form needs one.

What is the barcode on the label?

It is the old USPS POSTNET code for the ZIP (or ZIP+4), including its check digit, drawn as tall and short bars.

Can I use these addresses for shipping or sign-ups?

No. This tool is for software testing and demos only. Do not use it for fraud, impersonation, or to get around verification on real services.

StreetSample produces sample data for testing. Names, phone numbers, emails and birthdays are invented. It does not check whether mail can be delivered to an address.
