How fancy text works

These are not fonts. Each letter is swapped for a different Unicode character that looks styled, so the result stays fancy wherever you paste it.

  1. Type your text in the box above.
  2. Browse the styles, or filter by Bold, Cursive, Cool or Decorated.
  3. Press Copy and paste it into your bio, nickname or message.
  4. Press ☆ to save a style to Favorites on this device.

Most bold, italic and script letters come from the Mathematical Alphanumeric Symbols block, U+1D400–U+1D7FF. Circled letters come from Enclosed Alphanumerics, U+2460–U+24FF.

Questions

Will it work on Instagram, Free Fire and WhatsApp?

Yes, for most styles. Instagram, WhatsApp, TikTok and Discord show nearly all of them. Games like Free Fire and PUBG limit name length and block some symbols, so pick a shorter style if a name is rejected.

Why do some letters show as boxes?

The device viewing the text has no font for that character. Older Android phones miss some rare symbols. Bold, italic and script styles are the safest choice.

Why are some letters left normal?

Unicode does not have every letter in every style. Small caps has no styled "x", for example, so the plain letter is kept.

Can screen readers read fancy text?

Often not well. Screen readers may spell out symbol names. Keep important information in plain text.

Is my text sent anywhere?

No. Everything is converted inside your browser.
