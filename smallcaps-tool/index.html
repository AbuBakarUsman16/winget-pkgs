Questions

What is small caps text?

Small caps are capital letters drawn at the height of lowercase letters, like ᴛʜɪꜱ. This tool uses Unicode characters that look like small capitals, so the effect survives copy and paste.

Where can I use it?

Instagram and TikTok bios, Discord names and messages, X posts, WhatsApp, YouTube comments, game nicknames and most other apps that accept Unicode.

Why do some letters look different or show as boxes?

A few platforms or old phones lack fonts for rare Unicode characters. Try another style if a letter shows as a box. Screen readers may also read these characters oddly, so avoid them for important information.

Is it free? Is my text saved?

It is free. Conversion runs in your browser; your text is never sent anywhere.
